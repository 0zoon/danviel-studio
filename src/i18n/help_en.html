This is how you get from raw stems to a finished song. Every step has a picture with golden numbers: the number in the picture shows where the control is, the note on the right says what it does. Click a picture to see it large.

5 steps to a finished song

⤓① LoadStems, ZIP, demo or split a song🎚② MixAuto-Mix, tracks, effects, recording, build tracks✨③ MasterLoudness, bass, reference, compare🎬④ Video & CoverHook, video, lyrics, cover studio📦⑤ ReleaseExport, versions, release package

① Load

  • Drop in stems (MP3, WAV, FLAC …), a ZIP or a whole folder. ZIPs inside ZIPs are unpacked too.
  • Suno: when a stem comes as WAV, MP3 and MIDI, the studio uses the WAV. MIDI files are added as their own synth tracks (switched off, to layer in).
  • Only have a finished song? “Split song into stems” separates it into vocals, drums, bass and the rest with AI. The progress shows right under the buttons.
  • To try things out: pick a demo song and load it. 15 little songs with intro, verse, hook, break and outro, about 1 to 1.5 minutes each: pop, R&B, trap, house, EDM, drill, lo-fi, reggaeton, ballad, afrobeats, amapiano, phonk, dancehall, synthwave and boom bap. Every demo comes as separate stems (drums, bass, keys, pad, lead, FX) to mix and practise with.
  • Nothing is switched on after loading. “↺ Last settings” brings back your last mix for these stems.

② Mix

Auto-Mix & Genre

  • Auto-Mix is a switch: the green button, ✓ = on. Click again = everything back to the original stems. Noisy vocal and instrument stems get denoised automatically.
  • The first row also has drill, afrobeats and lo-fi. Under “More genres” you find instrumental, classical, cinematic, game, background music and jazz, plus reggaeton, K-pop, amapiano, gospel, country, techno, drum & bass, psytrance, synthwave, phonk and metal. What changes per genre is in the table below.
  • Pick a genre or “Automatic” (listens to the song). If the song comes from the Suno archive, the genre is already pre-selected.
  • Own genres: “+ Own genre” starts from an existing genre and you only change what should be different (level per track group, reverb, echo, compression, pumping, loudness, width, tone, every value in the pro section). “From my mix” turns your fine-tuned Auto-Mix into a new genre. Own genres sit under “Own”, the pencil edits them. Export as .json to share, “Import” or just drop the file into the window. They are saved in the browser and in the project. “Automatic” only picks built-in genres.
  • Reverb in Auto-Mix: Off (default, Suno vocals already have reverb), A little or Normal.
  • The small buttons (Auto-Balance, Auto-EQ …) each do just one part. “Reset mix” starts over.
  • 🎬 TikTok clip: one click finds the hook, sets 9:16 and jumps to the video.
  • Quick tweaks: 🎤 vocals louder / quieter, 🔊 more bass, 🥁 more punch, 📻 radio mix, ↔ wider, 🧼 clean (no effects), 🔥 warmer, ✨ brighter. Every click works on the current mix, Ctrl+Z takes it back.

Tracks

  • Switch on the left = track on/off, S = hear only this track (solo).
  • 🔊 Volume: drag the slider or use − / + (1 dB each). Double-click the slider = 0 dB.
  • Sound opens everything for this track: panning, low cut, bass, highs, space EQ, compressor, level rider, de-esser, remove noise, reverb, echo.
  • ✂ Trim & move (top of “Sound”): zoom in, cut the start and end with the yellow handles, make the track come in earlier or later. The original is kept.
  • ✎ or double-click the name = rename the track.
  • ━ Sliders / ◉ Knobs: show every control as a rotary knob like on a mixing desk. Turn = drag up or down, mouse wheel, Shift = fine, double-click = default value.
  • ⚙ Pro view opens all controls on every track at once.

Effects

  • Effects have their own switch and always start off, so nothing sounds “chopped” before you want it.
  • Reverb: room, plate or hall. Echo runs in time automatically. Sidechain ducks bass or music on every kick.
  • “Auto vocal FX” puts fitting reverb and echo on the voice.

Tempo & key

  • Keep pitch: only the tempo changes. Tape: slower = lower, faster = higher.
  • One click: 🐢 Slowed + Reverb, ⚡ Sped Up, 🌙 Nightcore, ↺ Original.
  • Key: −2, −1, Original, +1, +2 semitones or the slider.

🎙 Recording

  • Put on headphones, then “● Record”. The song plays along (2 s pre-roll or from the section) and you sing or rap over it.
  • Every take becomes its own track (“Take 1” …) with all mix controls. “Offset” nudges it in time, 🗑 deletes it.
  • With no song loaded you record freely and build your song from scratch.

🧱 Build a track

  • Add your own tracks without any experience: everything runs in time with your song automatically (tempo and first kick are detected). Without a song you set tempo and length at the top and build a beat from scratch.
  • 🥁 Drum pattern: starts with a template that fits the genre. Click or drag over boxes to set hits, click a name (kick, snare …) to hear the sound. Pattern 1, 2, 4 or 8 bars long. With drum pads (e.g. Akai MPD) or a keyboard you play the drums straight in: hit = listen, while the song plays every hit lands in the pattern, snapped to the 16th.
  • Samples: 40 one-shots in groups (drums, 808 & bass, crowd shouts like “Hey!” and “Yeah!”, hits like air horn and orchestra hit, FX like riser and impact, atmo like vinyl crackle and rain). Click = listen, “+ Drums” puts the sample into the drum pattern as its own row. Every sound is made in the studio and free to use. Under “Your own” you load your own one-shots, they stay in your browser and travel with the saved project.
  • 🔁 Loop / sample: load an audio file (up to 60 s). It is fitted to the song tempo and placed on whole bars.
  • 📍 Timeline: your song at the top, every built track below. Click or drag over bars = on or off. “Whole song”, “Section only” (e.g. just the hook) or “Nowhere” in one click.
  • ⤵ Put into track: mix a built track into an existing track instead of keeping it as its own. Undo at the target track under “Sound”.
  • Every built track is a normal track afterwards: volume, sound, effects, Auto-Mix, export. “✎ Edit” on the track opens it again.
  • 🎹 Instrument: choose a sound, then play: with the mouse on the keys, with the computer keyboard (A W S E D F T G Y H U J K = one octave) or with a USB keyboard (Chrome/Edge). Just plug it in: the studio detects it right away and offers to create an instrument track. Velocity, sustain pedal and pitch bend work, the start/stop button on the keyboard starts recording.
  • ● Record: one bar count-in with metronome, then the song runs and you play your pattern. “Snap” puts the notes exactly on the beat. Recording again adds new notes.
  • Piano roll: click an empty spot = add a note, drag a note = move, drag its right edge = longer, double-click or right-click = delete.

③ Master

  • Effects (reverb, echo, sidechain) and mastering have their own switches and start off.
  • Mastering goal: Natural, For Spotify & YouTube (−14 LUFS) or Loud (club & DJ).
  • Bass boost: More, Lots, Maximum or 🔊 Bass Boosted (the old, overdone YouTube bass).
  • Reference song: load a song that sounds the way you want. Loudness and tone are matched to it.
  • Hear before: the original without mix and mastering, to compare.

④ Video & Cover

Section & hook

  • Find hook looks for the chorus and starts just before the drop. 15 / 30 / 45 / 60 s = length.
  • Zoom: mouse wheel over the waveform. Swipe sideways or Shift + mouse wheel scrolls through the song. Plus ＋ / －, “Whole song” and “To section”.
  • “Snap to bar”: on = the section always jumps to whole bars. Off = anywhere, even between beats. To place it exactly: zoom in and untick it.
  • Drag the section = move it, drag its edges = longer/shorter, ◀ 1 bar ▶ = move by one bar.
  • “Section only” in the export: mix and video use only this part.

Video

  • Format: 9:16 (TikTok, Reels, Shorts), 1:1 or 16:9 (YouTube). The preview runs live while you play.
  • Picture: “Cover in the middle” or “Full screen (corner to corner)”, then your picture fills the whole video with a slow zoom. Under “In the video” you switch title, visualizer and lyrics on or off with one click. In full screen you move the visualizer and lyrics right in the preview and drag the gold corner to make them bigger or smaller, “Back to default” resets everything. Without lyrics the cover gets bigger automatically.
  • Karaoke lyrics: the line is highlighted as it is sung and stops where the lead vocal pauses, even if an instrumental part follows. With the “Karaoke sweep” switch off the whole line shows right away.
  • Background video (under “More settings”): choose a clip or drag it onto the preview. Show or hide visualizer, cover and title one by one.
⭐ Your own video with your song

You shot or made a video, on your phone, in CapCut or with a video AI? Just drop it onto the preview. The studio puts your finished song under it and the lyrics in time and your title on top. Out comes a finished clip for TikTok, Reels or YouTube.

  1. Drop the video onto the preview (or “Choose video”).
  2. Pick the format: 9:16 for TikTok and Reels, 16:9 for YouTube.
  3. For a pure video look switch off visualizer and cover, only the lyrics stay.
  4. Optional: “Find hook” and “Only section”, then it becomes a Short.
  5. “Create video (MP4)”. If the video is shorter than the song, it keeps looping.
  • Visualizer styles: Classic, Neon, Cyberpunk, Late Night R&B, Pastel Pop, Minimal or your own (right below: “Build or import your own style”).
  • Own branding and logo sit to the right of “Create video”. Your branding goes to the top, the DANVIEL mark then moves to the top right. The signature “Danviel Studio” always sits bottom right.
  • Title: choose position, alignment, font, title and artist size, or drag it in the preview with the mouse, the gold corner resizes it. Move the cover or record, lyrics and visualizer the same way (everything snaps to the middle), they always stay in the frame and never cover the watermark or signature. If the artist is DANVIEL, it appears as in the logo. With the keyboard: Tab into the preview, N selects the next element, arrow keys move it (Shift for bigger steps), + and − change the size.
  • The cover from the cover studio (with text and filters) goes into the video automatically.
  • Tip: for Instagram, export in Chrome or Edge (normal AAC audio).

Lyrics & Suno archive

  • Detect lyrics: the AI listens to the vocals. Or add your own lyrics, then “Fit to the vocals automatically” or “Tap the start, rest automatic” (tap only the first 3 lines, the automatic does the rest) or “Tap along” (space bar at every line, clock with milliseconds, −100 / +100 ms to nudge).
  • A word misheard? Click ✎ in the scrolling list (or double-click the line) and fix it right there.
  • Suno archive: a small script program (by a friend) that saves two text files per Suno song into your folder: “Title.txt” (prompt, style, genre, settings) and “Title - Lyrics.txt” (only the sung text).
  • Click “📂 Connect Suno archive” once and choose the folder. When you then load a song with the same title, the studio takes over title, artist, lyrics and genre right away. The genre is only pre-selected for Auto-Mix, Auto-Mix stays off until you switch it on. BPM and key are shown as info.
  • Without connecting: just drop the two text files in together with the stems.

Cover studio

  • ✍ Cover prompt from the lyrics (button in the lyrics section or at the top of the cover studio): your whole lyrics become an image prompt with a fitting main motif, light, colours and a big readable title.
  • “✨ Create cover prompt” writes it into the field for free (even more reliable with your own OpenAI key). “Copy prompt” → paste into ChatGPT → ChatGPT paints the cover. “🔄 New concept” = a different motif.
  • “📋 Straight to ChatGPT”: copies a brief that makes ChatGPT read the lyrics and paint the cover in one step.
  • If title or artist is missing, the studio asks first. Without lyrics it can’t work.
  • Create a cover with AI or drop in your own picture or paste it with Ctrl+V (e.g. from ChatGPT).
  • Fonts with preview: the font menu shows your title in every font, hover and you see it big on the cover right away, a click takes it. Over 75 fonts in groups (rap & trap, elegant, handwriting, retro & neon, clean & modern), with search and ★ favourites. “Add picture” sits right under the preview.
  • Title and artist: position, over 75 fonts, size, many colours plus a custom colour, outline, upper/lower case, letter spacing, and drag them right in the picture.
  • Filters: brightness, contrast, colour, warmth, hue, fade, blur, glow, RGB split (VHS), light leaks, grain, duotone, sepia, black & white.
  • Save as PNG in 3000 × 3000 (ready for Spotify). The finished cover also shows up in the video.

⑤ Release

  • Export mix as MP3, WAV or M4A, optionally only the section with fade in/out. Leave the sample rate on original or set 44.1 kHz (CD, streaming) or 48 kHz (video). 16-bit WAV gets a quiet dither so fade-outs stay clean, 24-bit WAV is for mastering and distribution.
  • Single stems, versions (instrumental, TV mix, acapella …) as a ZIP, kick as MIDI.
  • Release check checks loudness, true peak, clipping, mono and silence.
  • Release package: master, instrumental, acapella, cover, lyrics (LRC/SRT), Suno prompt and Shorts video in one ZIP.
  • Save project (ZIP): everything in one file: tracks, settings, cover and all cover drafts, the finished cover with text, logo, lyrics, background video and your finished videos. The recipient just drops it in and has everything back.

📦 Packs

  • The teal “📦 Packs” buttons at Auto-Mix, effects, mastering, and visualizer styles: ready-made settings with one click. Mastering (e.g. “Club Punch”), effects (e.g. “Trap Space”) and chains for single tracks (e.g. “Trap Lead Vocal” on your vocal track, “Deep 808” on the bass).
  • Packs go on top: “Deep 808” gives the bass more sub but resets nothing else. Levels, balance and loudness from Auto-Mix stay (only “loud” packs like “Club loud” raise the loudness). After the click you see what changed, with “Undo”. Active packs are remembered and added on top again after every new Auto-Mix, × removes one.
  • The DANVIEL Starter Pack is included. Load more packs (.json) with “📂 Load pack” or drop them into the window.
  • Your own pack: save your current mastering, effects, a track’s chain and your visualizer and cover styles as a file and share it.
  • Packs contain only settings, no code. Real VST plugins come with DANVIEL Studio Pro.

Monitor

  • On wide screens the monitor follows you on the right: a big live preview of the video, the player with waveform, loudness and analyzer, and “Selected” with the most used controls of the track you touched last.
  • The gear picks the design (stainless steel, chrome, black glass, leather) and shows or hides modules. Move them with the handle, video and player always stay.

Good to know

  • Every section has “Expand / Collapse” at its top right, folded it shows in one line what's inside. When you scroll down, a mini player with volume appears at the bottom.
  • Save, open and delete the project at the very bottom of the folder. Rest the mouse on a button and a short explanation shows right away.
  • Keys 1–5 (number pad too) jump straight to the steps. A = Auto-Mix, M = mastering on/off, B = before/after, L = load files, V = create video, E = export. ? or the keyboard button on the left of the step bar shows all shortcuts.
  • Background: over 25 looks, e.g. carbon, walnut, studio wood wall, vinyl, night city, aurora, gold lines, matte leather … or “📷 Own photo”, which stays in your browser only.
  • Space bar starts and stops anywhere. Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y = undo / redo.
  • If something doesn't work: “Save error log” at the very bottom and send the file.
  • All news is under “📋 Changelog” at the top.
